Accessibility Statement

Last updated: 2026

SwayForm wants the website and Learning Hub to be usable by everyone, including principals, instructors, and students using assistive technology.

What we aim for

  • Correct heading hierarchy so screen readers can navigate content logically.
  • Keyboard-accessible navigation and visible focus states on interactive elements.
  • Sufficient color contrast between text and backgrounds.
  • Accessible form labels on the Contact Sales form and other inputs.
  • Descriptive alt text on meaningful images.
  • Reduced-motion support — the interactive robot anatomy viewer falls back to a simple cross-fade instead of video transitions when a visitor's system requests reduced motion.
  • Semantic buttons and links, and accessible copy-code buttons in the Learning Hub.
  • Responsive layouts, including mobile-friendly tables and navigation.

Known limitations

This site is under active development. Some pages may not yet fully meet every guideline above. We are working toward full conformance and will update this statement as issues are fixed.

Reporting an issue

If you encounter an accessibility barrier on this website or in the Learning Hub, please tell us at contact@swayform.net — include the page URL and, if possible, the assistive technology you were using. We will follow up.
