From the Founder
SR

"I've seen how many people are afraid that robots and AI will replace them. I believe the best way to change that fear is through understanding. These technologies are not just replacing jobs. They are creating new industries, new careers, and new opportunities.

Students should not have to wait until college or the workforce to understand how robotics works. They deserve hands-on experience in high school, where they can build, test, and see their ideas work in the real world. SwayForm is helping shape an education system that prepares students for the future instead of asking them to catch up to it."

Swayam Rathod
Founder, SwayForm
