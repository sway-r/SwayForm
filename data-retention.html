Data Retention & Deletion Policy

Last updated: September 9, 2026 · Version 2026-09-09

This page explains how long SwayForm LLC keeps different kinds of data described in our Privacy Policy and Student & Children's Privacy Notice, and how to request deletion. We keep data only as long as reasonably necessary for the purpose it was collected for, then delete or de-identify it.

How to request deletion

  • Students and parents: contact your school first where possible (your school can reach us on your behalf and verify your identity/authority quickly), or email contact@swayform.net directly.
  • Schools: an authorized school representative (the account holder who signed up, or an admin listed on the account) can email contact@swayform.net to request deletion of an individual student's data or the entire school's data.
  • We will verify that a deletion request is coming from an authorized person before acting on it, consistent with how COPPA and Georgia's student-data law expect these requests to be handled.

Backups and residual copies

Like most services, SwayForm maintains routine backups for disaster-recovery purposes. Deleting a record from our live database does not instantly delete every historical backup — backups are retained for a limited window (up to 30 days) and are not restored or mined for individually deleted records; they simply age out and are overwritten on our provider's normal schedule.

Contact

Questions about this policy, or a deletion request, can be sent to contact@swayform.net.

SwayForm is in pilot development. As our infrastructure and school base grow, we intend to build a dedicated admin tool for a school to trigger its own full data export/deletion, rather than relying solely on a manual request to this email address.
