Cloud Classroom

You own the robot, but a robot can only be controlled by one person at a time. Cloud Classroom enrolls your school on our hosted server, so up to 15 students can be active at once and up to 60 students can have accounts. It is free for the first 3 years, then $149 a year.

Simulation after year 3

Simulation is free for the first 3 years and $500 a year after that. It is completely optional: with the real robot in the classroom, students don't need a simulation to see their code run.

The same path students see in the Learning Portal.

Each section follows one behavior, such as a wave, from its first line of code to a full program. Early labs teach one small piece. Later labs add concepts and how the robot is built and why. By the middle of a section students understand most of it, and by its last lab they write enough of the code on their own.

Students write more of the code in each section: about 30% in the first section, about 55% by the seventh, and about 75% by the tenth. Lab titles will be finalized as development continues.
