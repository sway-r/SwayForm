Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 9, 2026 · Version 2026-09-09

This Privacy Policy is issued by SwayForm LLC ("SwayForm," "we," "us," or "our"), based in Atlanta, Georgia. It explains how we collect, use, store, and share information across the SwayForm website (swayform.net), the Learning Portal (learning.swayform.net), our administrative tools, and the S1 robot platform.

If you are a student, parent, guardian, or school employee, please also read the Student & Children's Privacy Notice, which covers COPPA, FERPA, and Georgia student-data law in more detail and controls over this policy for anything specific to student data. Schools should also see the School Data Agreement, and anyone interested in our security practices should see the Security page. Retention and deletion specifics live on the Data Retention & Deletion Policy page. Physical safety and acceptable use of S1 hardware is covered separately by the Robot Safety and Acceptable Use Policy.

1. Scope of this policy

This policy covers every SwayForm-operated surface a school, family, student, or visitor might interact with:

  • The marketing website at swayform.net and www.swayform.net
  • The Learning Portal at learning.swayform.net, including student, teacher, and administrator accounts
  • Administrative dashboards used to manage schools, robots, and student rosters
  • The S1 robot platform itself, including on-device processing and any connection it makes to the Learning Portal
  • Support and sales interactions, including our contact forms

SwayForm is currently in a pilot development stage — we do not yet process commercial purchases through this website, and some sections below describe both what is true today and how a feature will be handled once it exists. Where that distinction matters, we say so explicitly rather than describing a feature we have not built yet.

2. The S1 platform

S1's core processing — motion control, ROS 2 nodes, and camera/sensor processing — runs locally on its onboard computer. S1 connects to the school's Wi-Fi (for the Learning Portal, classroom accounts, and updates), but does not require a cloud connection to operate.

  • Camera and sensor processing happens locally on S1 by default.
  • As of the date of this policy, classroom images and video are not uploaded to SwayForm's servers automatically, are not stored by SwayForm, and are not recorded by SwayForm.
  • Classroom data is not sold.
  • Classroom data is not used to train external or general-purpose AI models by default.

The optional cloud simulation subscription (see For Schools) is a separate, opt-in service — it is not required for S1 to operate. Enabling it requires separate disclosure and consent before it is turned on.

If SwayForm later introduces a feature that transmits, relays, or stores camera or video data off of the robot — for example, a live classroom viewing feature — this policy will be updated before that feature is enabled for any school, with a clear description of what is transmitted, who can view it, whether it is ever recorded, and how long any recording is kept. We will not silently expand what leaves the robot without updating this section first.

3. Cameras, biometrics, and AI

SwayForm does not use facial recognition, voiceprint recognition, or gesture-based biometric identification, and does not create biometric identifiers (as defined by applicable law, including the amended COPPA Rule) from classroom images, video, or sensor data. This is true even though S1's camera and depth sensor are technically capable of supporting such features in the future.

If SwayForm ever adds a biometric-recognition feature, it will be a separate, clearly disclosed, opt-in feature requiring its own authorization process — never a silent addition to an existing product.

SwayForm does not send Student Data (defined below), classroom video, audio, or student-written code to third-party AI services to train general-purpose AI models. We may use properly de-identified or aggregated information (for example, anonymized feature-usage counts) to understand how our products are used, diagnose performance issues, and improve our educational products — but we will not attempt to re-identify properly de-identified Student Data.

4. Student data

"Student Data" means personal information about a K-12 student that is collected or generated in connection with SwayForm's products, including but not limited to: name, school-affiliated or personal email address, school name, account identifiers, class or robot-seat assignments, lesson progress, activity logs, code or projects a student creates, and any information provided by the school on the student's behalf.

  • SwayForm does not sell Student Data.
  • SwayForm does not use Student Data for targeted or behavioral advertising, and does not build advertising profiles from it.
  • SwayForm processes Student Data only to provide, maintain, and improve the educational service the school has requested — not for unrelated commercial purposes.
  • Where a school provides or authorizes access to Student Data, SwayForm acts as a school service provider with respect to that data, consistent with FERPA's "school official" exception: we use Student Data only for the purposes authorized by the school, do not redisclose it except as the school authorizes or as law requires, and maintain it under our own direct security obligations to the school.

A school's authorization does not make the school solely responsible for compliance with COPPA or other student-privacy laws. SwayForm maintains its own obligations as an operator under COPPA and other applicable law regardless of any school consent or authorization it relies on.

See the Student & Children's Privacy Notice for the full COPPA notice, including what is collected from children under 13, parental and school review/deletion rights, and how to make a request.

6. Signing in with Google

The Learning Portal uses Google Identity Services ("Sign in with Google") rather than a username/password system. When you sign in this way, Google provides us with a signed token containing your email address, whether that email is verified, your display name, and your profile picture — nothing more. We do not request any additional Google permissions (such as access to your Drive, Calendar, or contacts), and we never receive your Google password.

8. We do not sell data or use it for advertising

SwayForm does not sell personal information of any kind — student or otherwise — and does not share it with third parties for their own marketing purposes. We do not use website visitor or Learning Portal account information for targeted or behavioral advertising, and we will not add contact-form submitters to a marketing mailing list.

10. Your choices and rights

  • Access and correction. You (or, for a student, your school or parent/guardian where applicable) can ask us what personal information we hold and request that inaccurate information be corrected.
  • Deletion. You can request deletion of your account and associated data at contact@swayform.net. For students, we may direct the request through the school first, consistent with how COPPA and Georgia's student-data law expect these requests to be verified and routed — but SwayForm will still assist and will not treat this as solely the school's responsibility.
  • No marketing use. We will not add you to a marketing list or use your information for advertising without your separate, affirmative consent.
  • Other states. This policy is currently written primarily with Georgia law in mind, since Georgia is SwayForm's home state and first target market. As SwayForm serves schools in other states, we will publish state-specific addenda addressing any additional rights those states provide.

11. Security

We maintain administrative, technical, and organizational safeguards designed to protect the information described in this policy, including access controls, encrypted connections, and least-privilege access to production data. Full detail is on the Security page. No system can be guaranteed to be 100% secure, and we do not claim otherwise. If we become aware of a security incident affecting your personal information, we will investigate and provide notifications required by applicable law and any agreement we have with your school.

12. Business transfers

If SwayForm is involved in a merger, acquisition, financing, or sale of assets, personal information may be transferred as part of that transaction. Any successor will remain subject to the commitments in this policy with respect to information collected before the transfer, including the student-data protections described here and in the Student & Children's Privacy Notice.

13. Changes to this policy

We will update the version number and "Last updated" date at the top of this page whenever this policy changes. For material changes — particularly anything affecting Student Data, camera/video handling, or retention — we will provide advance notice (for example, to school administrators on file) before the change takes effect where practical.

14. Contact

Questions, access requests, or deletion requests can be sent to contact@swayform.net. SwayForm LLC is based in Atlanta, Georgia.

SwayForm is in pilot development and expanding this policy as our products develop — for example, as the optional cloud simulation subscription and future robot connectivity features are finalized. Material changes will be reflected on this page with an updated date and version number.
