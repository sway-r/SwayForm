Our Mission

Bring real robotics into every classroom.

Students code a real robot and watch it respond. No robotics background needed.

Why it matters

Students should not be left behind by the tools shaping tomorrow.

Every generation grows up with tools that reshape how work gets done: calculators, computers, and now robotics and AI. The problem isn’t that the technology exists. It’s when students only hear about it from a distance.

SwayForm lets them test it, build with it and improve it, connecting code to real physical behavior.

Robots will change jobs, and create new ones: designing, programming and guiding these systems.

How we teach

Learning that stays close to the machine.

Students understand things better when the real system is right in front of them. On S1, the code, sensors and motion all run on the physical robot.

Each lab is short and hands-on: read a little, change a line, run it, and watch what happens. That direct link between what you write and what the machine does is the whole point.

S1 connects to the school’s Wi-Fi like any other device, with a one-time setup.

The real goal

The future should be built by people who understand it.

The goal isn’t just to teach robotics. It’s to help students become confident around the systems that will shape their future.

A student who learns how a robot senses, moves and responds isn’t just using technology. They’re learning how to think with it.

Practical, responsible and physical. Built for programs that last, with repairable hardware and modular labs.
